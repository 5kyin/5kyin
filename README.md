## The product

HushPOS is a point-of-sale experience built around the everyday realities of running a cafe.

From taking orders to managing cafe operations, the goal is simple: make the important things work together without unnecessary complexity.

## Loyalty without surveillance.

Traditional loyalty programs often ask customers to exchange personal information for rewards.

We believe there is another way.

**Customers should own their identity, not hand it over to earn a coffee.**

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/hushpos-loyalty.png" width="100%" alt="Privacy-first BrewOS loyalty QR pass"/>
</div>

### How it works

| Step | Experience |
|:--|:--|
| **01 — First order** | A random 256-bit secret is generated. No name or phone number required. |
| **02 — QR pass** | The customer receives a QR code containing their secret. |
| **03 — Return visit** | The QR code is scanned to restore loyalty continuity. |
| **04 — Rewards** | Points accumulate against the secret, not a personal profile. |

<blockquote>
  Loyalty should reward customers, not collect them.
</blockquote>

## What we're building

- **Point of sale** — Counter and table ordering workflows.
- **Cafe operations** — Tools for managing everyday workflows.
- **Inventory intelligence** — Ingredient costing, stock tracking and consumption-based accounting.
- **Privacy-first loyalty** — A QR-based customer identity without requiring conventional personal details.

---

## Engineering philosophy

HushPOS is built around a few principles:

- **Privacy by design** — Minimize unnecessary personal data collection.
- **Operational clarity** — Make everyday workflows understandable.
- **Accounting integrity** — Track actual inventory consumption and costs.
- **Reliable systems** — Prefer explicit business logic and maintainable architecture.
